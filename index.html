About the project

“The future of work will not be determined by technology alone.”

Gilbert F. Houngbo

Work with change, not against it.

Houngbo’s human-centred approach to technological progress connects productivity gains with decent wages, worker protections, skills development and social dialogue. These conditions help ensure technological innovation supports decent employment.

For young people facing insecure entry-level work, these choices shape who gets access to opportunity. NEET status and Tang Ping describe distinct circumstances. NEET describes being outside employment, education or training. Tang Ping, or “lying flat,” describes a way of responding to social and economic pressures. Both bring questions of exclusion, effort and reward into focus.

Drawing on the Daoist philosophy of Wu Wei, the two artworks explore how to adapt while retaining creative agency. One folds volume into a changing sculpture. The other lets interwoven shapes form new relationships where they meet.

Sustainable Development Goal 8

Decent Work and Economic Growth

SDG 8 calls for inclusive, sustainable economic growth, productive employment and decent work for all. For young people entering creative industries, it asks how technological change can expand access to secure, fairly paid work.
